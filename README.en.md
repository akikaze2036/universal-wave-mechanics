## About This Theory

*4D Invariant Matrix Cosmology* set out to explain the universe through waves.  
Through ongoing verification and discussion, that idea was refined and expanded from the cosmic stage—the "Matrix"—to wave behavior and actions across all things, forming a unified framework in the main treatise.

At its core, all existence is variations in energy density, behaving as waves.  
From elementary particles and forces to atoms, living organisms, the Earth, the solar system, galaxies, and the universe itself—everything is a manifestation of this single wave nature.  
**Even the human being reading these words right now is nothing more than a collection of elementary particles that are themselves variations in energy density.**

Amid countless cosmological models, how far can one simple wave rule reach across physical phenomena? Put it to the test in the main treatise and the side story.

<br>

## *Universal Wave Mechanics* in Three Points

- **All things are waves**: Their substance is variations in energy density $u(x, y, z, \tau)$ across a 4D invariant space, together with actions governed by density and mass.
- **The Matrix and all things follow the same rules**: Both the space where universes emerge and decline (the Matrix) and the matter or life within it (all things) obey a single foundational equation.
- **Universes overlap as spheres of influence**: Rather than closed boxes, universes cycle through generation and decline as open wave ranges that interfere with one another.
